Math Toys

Small things to click on, by Paul Callahan

Hex Connect
Flip hexagonal Truchet tiles until every arc joins into one closed loop. Each board has an exact par, worked out against every one of the 1,848,868 single-loop tilings, and a hint if you get stuck.
Hex Truchet Loops
Flip hexagonal Truchet tiles until every arc joins into one closed loop. There's a solver if you get stuck.
Gray-Scott Explorer
Wander the parameter space of a reaction-diffusion system and watch spots, stripes and mazes grow.
Flexagon Strip Generator
Describe a flexagon and get a strip to print, cut and fold. Revived from a program I first wrote around 1990.
